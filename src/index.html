A decentralized healthcare platform that uses blockchain technology to securely manage electronic medical records. Services

This advanced healthcare platform harnesses blockchain technology to enhance medical data security and efficiency. It supports seamless sharing of patient information among healthcare providers and facilitates rapid, analytics-driven predictions of potential health issues. Additionally, the system ensures that sensitive patient data can be securely accessed by authorized proxies, enabling robust decision-making support even when patients cannot make decisions themselves.

Quick Disease Prediction
